About the project

Make good resources easier to discover.

The internet already contains an enormous amount of Shia educational, scholarly, cultural and digital material. The challenge is often finding it, understanding what it is for, and knowing where to start.

Our mission

Organize discovery, not replace the resources.

Shia Resource Hub is designed as a curated directory. We point people toward useful resources, explain what they offer, and organize them around real needs such as learning, research, books, Quran, Hadith, duas, apps and children’s education.

The project can grow with community input while keeping quality and clarity at the center.

Principles

CuratedResources should have a clear reason for being included.
UsefulEvery listing should tell visitors what they can actually use it for.
AccessibleWe aim to make discovery easier across languages, formats and experience levels.
Community-firstSuggestions can help the directory grow through a review process.
