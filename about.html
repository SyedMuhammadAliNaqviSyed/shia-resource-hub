About the project

Make good resources easier to discover.

The internet already contains a large amount of Shia educational, scholarly, cultural and digital material. The challenge is often finding it, understanding what it is for, and knowing where to start.

Our mission

Organize discovery, not replace the resources.

Shia Resource Hub is a curated directory. It points people toward useful existing resources, explains what they offer, and organizes them around real needs such as learning, research, books, Quran, Hadith, duas, apps and children’s education.

It is not a content dump, a social feed, or a religious authority. Quality and clarity come first. Community suggestions can help the directory grow through review.

Principles

CuratedResources should have a clear reason for being included.
UsefulEvery listing should tell visitors what they can actually use it for.
AccessibleDiscovery should work across languages, formats and experience levels.
Need-firstParents, youth, students and teachers should be able to start from a goal, not only a keyword.
Community-firstSuggestions help growth, but review protects quality.
