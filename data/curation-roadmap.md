# Curation Roadmap

The Hub should grow by usefulness, not by raw link count.

## Priority batches

### 1. Core reference
- Quran and tafsir
- Hadith databases
- Duas and ziyarat
- Major digital libraries
- Official marja / institution resources

### 2. Urdu and South Asia
- Urdu books and libraries
- Urdu lectures and educational platforms
- Pakistan-relevant community resources
- Urdu Android/iOS apps

### 3. Learning
- Beginner study paths
- Courses
- Lecture archives
- Structured curricula
- Teacher/parent resources

### 4. Kids and families
- Children's books
- Printables
- Activities
- Age-specific learning
- Family study resources

### 5. Research
- Searchable primary texts
- Academic journals and repositories
- Manuscript/catalogue resources
- Rijal and bibliographic tools
- Research utilities

### 6. Community and practical tools
- Prayer and calendar tools
- Qibla and location tools
- Community directories
- Event/resource platforms

## Curation target

Aim for a balanced, well-described collection rather than an arbitrary number such as 100 or 1,000. Every added resource should have a clear reason to exist in the directory and should pass the quality checklist where verification is claimed.
